world-countries.min.json — country outlines for LCM Map 2 (TKP Connect 0.3.0).

Built from world-atlas 2.0.2 (countries-110m.json), converted to GeoJSON with topojson-client 3,
coordinates rounded to 0.01°, Antarctica removed, rings crossing 180° shifted east (Russia, Fiji), and a label point (lat, lon) added per country.
